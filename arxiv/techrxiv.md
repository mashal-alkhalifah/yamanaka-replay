# TechRxiv submission

IEEE's preprint server, hosted on Figshare. Chosen after bioRxiv declined
the paper at screening (see `biorxiv.md`): it takes engineering and
computer-science work, which is what this paper is, and the biology is the
worked example rather than the contribution.

## Why this one

- **No endorsement and no sponsor**, and no IEEE membership requirement.
- **No fee.**
- Scope is unpublished technical content in engineering, computer science
  and related technologies. A complete research article qualifies;
  stand-alone code or a dataset would not.
- A **DOI** is issued on posting.
- Moderators screen for plagiarism, scope and whether the content is
  technical and scientific — not for merit, completeness or novelty. A
  decision usually arrives within **four business days**, and a decline can
  be appealed to `appeal@TechRxiv.org`.
- Only the author may submit; third-party submissions are declined. So the
  account and the final button are the author's.

It does **not** satisfy RECOMB's requirement that accepted authors post to
arXiv or bioRxiv. That is why arXiv is still being pursued in parallel —
see `endorsement.md`. The RECOMB notification is 22 December 2026, so there
is time.

Source: https://techrxiv.figshare.com/f/submission-guidelines
